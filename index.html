01 — ABOUT

On the ground, not just on paper.

A summary of who I am and how I got into solar.

I like problems with a right answer — correct array sizing, clean cable runs, an inverter configured the way the manual actually says to.

I'm a graduand of the BSc in Energy and Environmental Technology at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology, and a certified solar technician through Bondo Technical and Training Institute (Solar Training T2 — installation, design and sizing, testing and commissioning, and BOQ preparation).

I currently work as a technician and sales & marketing assistant at Sproma Energy under Dominic Osyanju, installing residential solar systems and representing the company at public events. Before that, at Phasian Consulting Limited, I mapped solar infrastructure and transmission networks across Kenya and researched waste management in the Nairobi Metropolitan area.
